## Interview Preparation Questions

- Why use a transparent gas in WSGG?
- Why does the WSGG superposition method multiply weights and add absorption coefficients?
- Why does the number of gray-gas combinations grow quickly in multi-species mixtures?
- What does `T_ref = 1400 K` accomplish numerically?
- Why can a gray Planck mean fail in non-isothermal absorbing media?
- What breaks when Rayleigh assumptions are applied to vegetation-scale particles?
- Which parts of this repository are workflow validation, and which would need real HAPI,
  FTIR, or FDS validation before being a research contribution?

## Honest Positioning

Use the portfolio to show numerical maturity and learning speed. Do not overclaim
combustion, spectroscopy, Fortran, FDS internals, or validated particle optics.
